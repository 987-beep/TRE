5. REFINED TECHNICAL EXPLANATION: HASH STALENESS VS THEORETICAL FIXED POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 7 refines the technical explanation previously termed the "Self-Referential
Hash Paradox":
- Mechanism: When a cryptographic hash H(D0) is computed on a document state D0,
  inserting that 64-character hex string into the document creates a modified state
  D1 = D0 + H(D0). Under SHA-256's cryptographic avalanche effect, H(D1) differs from
  H(D0) almost certainly. The embedded digest becomes immediately stale.
- Mathematical Qualification: Finding a self-referential fixed point where H(D*) = H*
  is mathematically conceivable but computationally infeasible under SHA-256 (requiring
  an expected 2^256 operations).
- Practical Engineering Remedy: External Manifest Accounting. Separating document
  payloads from their integrity metadata ensures deterministic, conflict-free verification.
