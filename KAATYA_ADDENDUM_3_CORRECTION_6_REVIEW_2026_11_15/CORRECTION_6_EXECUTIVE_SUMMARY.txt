3. Root Cause Analysis:
   This discrepancy was caused by the Self-Referential Hash Paradox.
   A document cannot embed its own plain-text SHA-256 hash inside its own body
   without altering its content and therefore altering its resulting hash.
   When the authoring script populated the table, it wrote the pre-calculated
   draft hash (Value A) into the text. Writing this string into the file altered its

   When CORRECTION_5_UPLOAD_MANIFEST.txt was generated post-creation, it accurately
   measured the physical file on disk (Value B). However, the internal text table
   retained the earlier draft string Value A.

4. Architectural Remedy:
   To completely eliminate self-referential hash paradoxes, Correction 6 enforces
   External Manifest Accounting. A document must never assert its own post-computation
   cryptographic checksum within its own body. Internal summary tables delegate
   document verification to the authoritative external manifest (CORRECTION_6_UPLOAD_MANIFEST.txt)
   and its detached cryptographic signature (CORRECTION_6_UPLOAD_MANIFEST.sha256).
